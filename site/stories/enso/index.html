01 / LOCATE

Begin with a window on the Pacific.

Niño 3.4 spans 170°W–120°W and 5°S–5°N. Its regional average helps describe ENSO conditions. It cannot tell you the temperature at every point inside it.

The highlight is a definition box; the CSV averages ocean points. Neighbouring Niño boxes overlap and cannot be summed into a global average.

03 / CONTEXT

How warm compared with the surrounding tropics?

The traditional index compares the region with its reference climate. The relative index also considers the tropical-ocean background and applies the producer's scaling convention.

This is not a fixed offset. A lower relative value does not mean the physical ocean has cooled. See Tim Stockdale's methodology.
